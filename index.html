CHAPTER ONE · A LITTLE NOTE

A letter, just because.

Some things are better opened slowly.

Tap the envelope to open it

My Baby ❤️, Sometimes I wonder how one person can become such a big part of another person's life. How someone can go from being just a person you know to becoming the first person you want to talk to, the one you miss in the middle of a random day, and the one whose smile can make everything feel a little better. Somehow, that's what you've become to me. I just want to see you happy when you're around me. I don't know if I tell you enough, but I am incredibly lucky to have you in my life. It's not just the big moments that make me love you. It's the little things — your voice, your cuteness, your silly talks, the way you care for me, and even those small moments when we're doing absolutely nothing but somehow everything feels good. I love all of it. Maybe, I'm not extraordinary. There will be days when I don't find the right words, when I get things wrong, or when I fail to express what's going on in my heart. But one thing I never want you to doubt is how much you mean to me. I want to keep learning you, understanding you, choosing you, and finding new ways to make you feel loved. Not just on special occasions, but on the most ordinary days too. I want that our love is a safe place, a source of comfort, and a reason to smile. It does not matter if it has been 8 years or 80 years, I want our love to stay alive, I want us to give each other reasons to fall in love again and again, and I want us to keep discovering new ways to make each other happy. I want us to have countless more memories together — spontaneous plans, long conversations, silly arguments that end in laughter, little adventures, lazy days, tight hugs, and those moments when we look at each other and don't need to say anything. I want to celebrate each other's achievements, stand beside each other through difficult days, and be someone we can always come home to emotionally. I want us to struggle together, grow together, have a great career, have an amazing love life, amazing sex life and a great family life. If I could make one promise, it would be this: I'll never stop trying to make our love feel special. I don't want us to just count the years; I want us to fill those years with moments worth remembering. I want that we keep falling in love with each other in different ways, at different stages of life, through every version of ourselves. Happy Anniversary, my love. Thank you for being you, for being my all-time, everytime person, and for making my world a little more beautiful just by being in it. And if life gave me a thousand chances to choose again, I'd still look for you. I love you more than you could ever know. ❤️

- Anurag Srigyan
